## What I do

I am most known for being a lead developer at [MASSGRAVE](https://github.com/massgravel). I was one of the people who researched and developed [TSforge](https://massgrave.dev/blog/tsforge), and I'm currently working on a full rewrite of the [MAS](https://github.com/massgravel/Microsoft-Activation-Scripts) project.

If you like the things I do, you can [support me](/donate). Anything helps, as I am still dependent on my family for income.
