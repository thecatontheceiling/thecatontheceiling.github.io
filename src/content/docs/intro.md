### Projects

Here are a few of them:

*  **[Microsoft Activation Scripts](https://github.com/massgravel/Microsoft-Activation-Scripts)**, a tool for managing Windows Activation that I am one of the developers of.
*  **[TSforge](https://github.com/massgravel/TSforge)**, now part of the above project.
*  **[DumbVersion](https://github.com/thecatontheceiling/DumbVersion)**, an extremely efficient binary diffing tool inspired by xdelta and SmartVersion.
*  **[The Definitive HITMAN 3 Patch](/hitman3patch)**, something I created to fight back against always-online bullshit in a game I otherwise really enjoy.
*  **[LyssaRDSGen](https://github.com/thecatontheceiling/LyssaRDSGen)**, a Remote Desktop Services keygen I wrote in 600 lines of vanilla JavaScript.
*  **[CLiPExploder](https://github.com/thecatontheceiling/CLiPExploder)**, an in-place decryptor for the ClipSp kernel driver included with Windows.
*  **[Shimbox](https://github.com/thecatontheceiling/shimbox)**, a hooking framework for the Xbox GDK equivalent of Steamworks.
*  **And many more...** Most are either unfinished, works in progress, or private. :)
