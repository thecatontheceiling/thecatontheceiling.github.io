## IV. My "Fine" School

Every day, when I go to school, I am met by my two classmates that also happen to be my only close friends IRL. I also have to awkwardly socialise with my other classmates, who are... let's say, not my type. Or, to put it in another way, mostly idiots. Not that I blame them for that, of course.

I also have a daily run-in with my school's director, who stands in front of the school entrance every morning for some reason.      

She decided this semester it would be in the school's and students' best interest to nuke all selection from the school's cafeteria and starve us for *exactly* the right amount of time to manage to go home and eat seconds before dying from malnutrition.        

Eating anywhere that isn't the cafeteria, which is an entire building and 5 flights of stairs away? Disallowed.     

Going outside the school for a few minutes to buy something that actually contains protein? Our other campus had a kid who got kidnapped while buying a KitKat or some shit and the school got fined, so no more of that.     

But who cares about not being starved to death? What about the lessons!? Yeah they're not any better.       

My math classes consist of material that I learnt in previous grades. Fucking triangles and finding angles and shit. We still haven't started calculus yet and I'm apparently 2 grades away from finishing school. Why?       

Obviously, the education system is different per-country (and sometimes per-school), but I feel like I somehow have it *better* than most schools in the world. At least my teachers are smart. At least they want us to think for ourselves and support us through everything. I doubt most schools have that. It is a shame.        

## V. The End

The education system in most countries is broken and, in my case, it is broken because of:
- The administrators at the top running my school.
- The teachers giving up due to low wages and *the fucking kids*.
- The actual education given not being good enough.

I hope that, by the time I am dead, most countries in the world will have significantly smarter populations and a better education system than now. I won't cross my fingers.       

For now, I am just waiting for my nonsensical suffering to end while I search for opportunities to get a proper education abroad (or a job? but maybe I'm deluding myself for thinking that could ever happen).
